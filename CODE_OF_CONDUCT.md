# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every space the project uses: issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and any other place where someone represents the project.

## In short

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and give and take feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or attention, or publishing of anyone's private
  information.
- Own mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

The linked text is the full statement of the standards and of what happens when they are not met.
Where this summary and that text differ, the linked text applies.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, write to **cameron@hosannaith.com**.

- Reports are read by the maintainer, Cameron Vickers.
- Your report is kept confidential. Details are shared only as far as needed to act on it.
- You will get an acknowledgement, and you will be told the outcome.

If your report concerns the maintainer, say so in your message and send it to
**security@hosannaith.com** as well, so that it is on record in a second place.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what action fits, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
